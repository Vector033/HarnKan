# Surface: HarnKan app (all screens)

Scope: the whole web app (home, join, trip with expenses / settle / members, sheets). Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: groups of 3–10 (students to families, mixed ages) recording shared costs on a phone during a trip and settling afterwards. Tasks: add or edit an expense in seconds, see who owes whom, pay via PromptPay QR, attach a slip, confirm money received, invite and manage members. Constraints: polite neutral Thai copy; never look like a bank app, childish, or a generic template; readable for older users; reduced motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every amount owed is a numbered coupon torn from the trip's own booklet. It refuses the fintech dashboard of white cards, a green accent and charts.

OWN-WORLD: Food-court coupon stock. Deep ink green-black (#14302b) shell and text on a cool pale ground (#eef1ef). Each member owns one coupon tint (rose, mint, sky, lemon, lilac, apricot), used for their avatar, payer chip and coupons. Red serial numerals (#c7352c) for coupon numbers only. Amounts in Kanit; UI text in Anuphan. Perforations are dashed borders; corners are small (8–10px), never pills.

STORY: The visitor sees the trip total and their own share at once, adds an expense with the big ink button, opens Settle and sees one coupon per transfer. They pay with the QR, attach the slip, and the receiver tears off the stub to confirm.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An ink header band with the trip name, member count and live status. Below it, two coupon stubs side by side (trip total, your share) carrying the trip's serial number. If you owe, a tear-off coupon follows (pay X, amount). Then the expense list with sticky day guide rows. An ink "+ เพิ่มรายจ่าย" button sits bottom right above a 3-tab bar.

FORM: Thai food-court coupon booklet, candidate 5 of 7 on the re-rolled grounded list (re-roll 1 on factual grounds: the merit envelope misrepresents debts as donations). seed:dfaba23147755d75. Raises: state is shown by line form (dashed = pending, solid = confirmed, struck = cancelled; from emission-line rail); rank comes from weight before size, a 4-step type scale (from timetable slide rack); sticky day guide words (from lexicon). Signature interaction: confirming a payment tears the coupon along its perforation into the history, in 280ms, reduced to a fade under reduced motion.
